Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy covers Arc Lake, Ash Tree IDE, Autumn, and DART Equation (the "Apps"), published by DART Meadow LLC / Radical Deepscale LLC ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

The Apps are designed to keep as much as possible on your device:

  • Simulation and project data (atoms, molecules, equation nodes, IDE projects) is stored locally on your device and, if you choose to sign in with iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud account. We do not have access to this data.
  • Sign-in with GitHub: if you connect a GitHub account, we use GitHub's standard OAuth flow to authenticate you. We store only the minimum needed to keep you signed in (your GitHub username and an access token), on your device.
  • Sign in with Apple: if offered and used, authentication is handled entirely by Apple; we receive only what Apple's sign-in flow provides.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details — Apple shares only whether a subscription is active.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports, if you choose to submit one through TestFlight or the App Store, are provided to us through Apple's standard feedback tools.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not run advertising or ad-tracking in the Apps.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the service providers named above (Apple, GitHub), each acting under their own privacy policies, solely to provide the functionality you requested (signing in, processing a purchase).

Data Retention & Deletion

Local and iCloud-synced data stays under your control — deleting the app or its iCloud data removes it. You can disconnect GitHub sign-in from within the app's profile screen at any time, which removes the stored access token from your device.

Children's Privacy

The Apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.

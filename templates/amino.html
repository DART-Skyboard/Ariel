Post-Translational Modifications

These are examples of standard amino acids that are chemically altered *after* the protein is built. These modifications are critical for function but are not part of the initial sequence.

Name Origin Key Role
Hydroxyproline Modification of Proline Critical for the structure and stability of collagen in skin and bone.
Phosphoserine Modification of Serine Acts as a molecular "on/off" switch for protein activity in cell signaling.

Non-Proteinogenic Amino Acids

These amino acids exist in the human body but are not used to construct proteins. They serve other vital roles.

Name Function Description
GABA Neurotransmitter A major inhibitory neurotransmitter in the brain, producing a calming effect.
Ornithine & Citrulline Metabolic Cycle Key intermediates in the urea cycle, helping to dispose of waste ammonia.
Taurine Various Plays roles in nerve function, heart health, and the formation of bile salts.

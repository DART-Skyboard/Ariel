What this page is showing

This instrument demonstrates the core passive mechanical operation of the Taurus itself. It is not a finished vehicle, and it is not a claim that a habitat contains only this part. In a real engineering product, other mechanical systems can be integrated, and many will already be present: structure, avionics, thermal control, docking, power storage, and whatever life-support hardware a crew still wants in reach. Those sit around the Taurus. They are not what is being shown here.

The Taurus is the key component. Its intended purpose is the delivery. A closed contour and cavity hold sea-level air, orient gravity on one wall, equalize the others, and filter the cabin gas with the enzyme byproduct of that same operation. The benefit of running it is a consistent habitat in which a person, an animal, or any other biology can thrive, without spinning the station and without a pump as the baseline. Power, solar, and the rest of the vehicle can be added. The purpose being delivered is the Taurus.

Keeping the chambers operating and connected takes additional hardware, and this page draws that layer as a holographic panel, the kind of overlay a crew would work in. Sensors on the six habitats do not need a powered network to agree. They correlate through pressure and temperature, which are already the passive facts of the mechanical operation. A difference in either one is felt by the others because they share the same gas and the same shell. The panel is where a crew then adjusts what the passive core does not decide by itself: pressure trim, how much filtration is held in the air, an introduced measure of gas, cabin temperature, and which wall carries gravity. Those are operations on top of the Taurus. They are not a second Taurus.

The torus is designed just right

Passive Taurus Pressure for Gravity is not a pump bolted to a tank, and it is not a station you spin. The body is the AJ10C chamber, but it is not being asked to burn. Combustion uses that contour as an intake and an output. Here the same contour and the same cavity are a closed passive path. Enzyme proportion, equalization, and the gravity pull on the cabin air travel through the cavity and return along the contour. The shape keeps the energy in the loop. Get the path right and you do not add a second system to do the rest.

Buoyancy in three orders

First is aerospace buoyancy. Outside the hull is vacuum, the root. A container holding sea-level air against that root is already buoyant, the way a hull is buoyant in water, except the outside fluid is nothing. Because aerospace buoyancy reaches the vacuum, it can operate the orders under it.

Second is maritime buoyancy. Fluid against fluid. On the walls that are not making gravity, a pendulum rides the torus contour, the opposed paths of the AJ10C chamber. Two bobs swing and return against each other, so the energy stays symmetric. That is the Reflex Potentials gear, run without a motor: aerospace pulls toward the median, maritime lifts, geometric holds the mass. The swing keeps the cabin air spread and points the pressure at the gravity wall. It does not pile the air there.

Third is geometric buoyancy. The container’s own volume. On the cubicle the gap between the shells is six habitats — deck, overhead, port, starboard, fore, aft — each sealed from the other five. Which wall is down is an assignment, not a spin. Any habitat can take gravity, return to equalization, or blend with another if down should meet a corner. The seals stay shut.

What Earth already does

A person on Earth does not feel “buoyant.” What they feel is that the atmosphere lets them live. The connection is bacteria and humidity at a surface: skin, the hide of an animal, the face of a machine. In the right humidity that living fraction is the proportion between the body and the gas. The air presses the surface. The same air is what they breathe. The system is already both mechanical and buoyant. It reacts against the body while it supplies the gas.

PTP rebuilds that loop on purpose, and passively. Enzymes and the bacterial fraction, held at the humidity of the cabin, react to the atmospheric gases. As needs change across the six habitats, the cabin, and the crew, equalization does not ask for more gas and does not throw gas away. The amount stays the amount. Sea-level pressure, 101.3 kilopascals, is the set point shared by the cabin and the habitat that is acting as gravity. The equality is with that side, not with the outer hull. The outer hull is structure against vacuum.

Used oxygen, cleaned in the proportion

Breath and bench work spend oxygen and leave carbon dioxide. The enzyme byproduct is already inside the mechanism, so it is a direct energy source for filtration. It does not have to be piped to a separate scrubber. The same motion that keeps equalization or gravity also takes the spent stream through the proportion and returns oxygen clean. Cabin pressure stays the sea-level set point, shared with the gravity side, and the gas stays spread. That is a consistent habitat. A person, an animal, or any other biology in the cabin is breathing the same air, at the same pressure, and the loop is what lets them thrive. Inventory does not rise to make up for a loss, and it does not fall because something was dumped.

Power, if you want it

The baseline is the buoyant pendulum, and the gases can keep it. Their byproduct is filtered energy, not a third gas and not a waste stream. One share is taken into the gear, so the passive path keeps moving. The rest is sent back into the same gases, which holds their direction: equalization on the walls that spread the air, gravity on the wall that orients the pressure. When that yield covers the draw, the loop is thriving and outside power is idle. A solar trickle or an enhancement can still add to it. A reflex pulse is only a temporary surplus. It decays back into the proportion. Sensors still see a loss or a gain of gravity, and a matched hitch does not throw people against the air.

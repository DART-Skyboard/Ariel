01 · OVERVIEW

ABOUT RADICAL DEEPSCALE LLC

Radical Deepscale LLC is an independent science, engineering, and artificial intelligence research organization operating across aerospace systems, computational physics, advanced AI architecture, and materials science. The company operates on a lean model — a single founder working in close collaboration with freelancers and researchers who share aligned interests in rigorous, long-horizon scientific development.

The DART Meadow platform is our public-facing product ecosystem, encompassing interactive engineering visualization tools, AI-driven interfaces, satellite tracking systems, physics simulation environments, and scientific design applications. Every product is built to be open, accessible, and durable — not designed for disposal.

This report represents our formal commitment to responsible operations, minimal environmental impact, and the core belief that building systems designed to endure is itself an act of sustainability.

Radical Deepscale's entire platform is served as static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript via GitHub Pages — one of the lowest-energy web infrastructure models available. There are no always-on application servers, no managed database instances, and no containerized compute clusters to power around the clock.

All content is delivered through GitHub's global CDN, serving files from data centers nearest to each user, reducing transmission distance, latency, and the energy cost per request. The elimination of server-side rendering removes compute-per-request overhead entirely.

AI inference is consumed through third-party API endpoints on a pay-per-use basis, meaning compute is provisioned only when actively needed — no idle GPU or CPU cycles are billed to this organization or burned on its behalf between sessions.

03 · PHILOSOPHY

LONGEVITY OVER DISPOSABILITY

The founding orientation of Radical Deepscale is optimization for the long run. This principle applies equally to physics, to code architecture, and to our relationship with the natural environment. We do not build to replace. We build to refine, extend, and endure.

Each application in the DART Meadow ecosystem is designed as a single-file progressive application — self-contained, with no external backend dependencies, minimal third-party CDN calls, and no forced obsolescence mechanisms. A tool built today is intended to function, without modification, years from now.

We view the engineering ethic of doing more with less as inseparable from environmental responsibility. Efficient algorithms, compact data structures, and lean rendering pipelines are not just performance goals — they are sustainability goals.

04 · PLEDGES

FORMAL SUSTAINABILITY COMMITMENTS

🌿 SERVERLESS FIRST

All platform tools will remain statically served wherever technically feasible. New backend infrastructure will only be introduced when no client-side alternative exists.

⚡ EFFICIENT COMPUTE

Algorithms, rendering systems, and AI prompt design will be continuously optimized to minimize token consumption, GPU cycles, and energy per operation.

🔁 DURABLE DESIGN

Products are built with zero planned obsolescence. Every tool is designed to remain functional and useful without mandatory version churn or forced migrations.

📂 OPEN BY DEFAULT

Core research, tools, and visualizations are published openly on GitHub Pages. Open access reduces duplication of effort across the global research community.

🤝 LEAN TEAM MODEL

Operating with a minimal permanent headcount and project-based freelance collaboration keeps overhead, commuting impact, and office resource consumption near zero.

🔬 RESPONSIBLE AI USE

AI tooling is used for research amplification, not replacement of rigorous scientific method. Prompt efficiency and output reuse are treated as environmental as well as cost considerations.

🌍 LONG-RUN ORIENTATION

Scientific and engineering decisions at Radical Deepscale are evaluated against long-term systemic outcomes, not short-term convenience — in alignment with our core mission statement.

📡 REMOTE OPERATIONS

All development, research, and collaboration is conducted remotely using lightweight digital tools, eliminating commute emissions and physical facility overhead.

05 · ALIGNMENT

RESEARCH AREAS & ENVIRONMENTAL RELEVANCE

MATERIALS SCIENCE · HERCULES ALLOY / T11-ZC
Advanced alloy research focused on high-performance, long-lifecycle materials for aerospace and tooling applications. Durable materials reduce replacement frequency and manufacturing throughput requirements over time.
COMPUTATIONAL PHYSICS · ARCLAKE / DART POTENTIALS
Particle physics and fluid dynamics simulation tools developed to run entirely in the browser using GPU compute — enabling complex scientific visualization without dedicated simulation server infrastructure.
AEROSPACE · DART HYPERSONIC EDGE
Hypersonic thermal management and aerospike propulsion research oriented toward next-generation propulsion efficiency. Higher specific impulse and thermal resilience translates to reduced fuel consumption per mission profile.
AI ARCHITECTURE · LEATR / AUTUMN / BRPN
The LEATR framework is designed around deterministic, formula-driven reasoning that minimizes redundant inference. Compact, efficient AI systems that reason more precisely require fewer compute cycles per output token.
SATELLITE & EARTH OBSERVATION · MANTIS RADAR
Real-time satellite tracking and Earth systems visualization tools support awareness of orbital resource management and climate monitoring infrastructure — areas directly tied to long-term planetary sustainability.
06 · FORWARD

FUTURE SUSTAINABILITY OBJECTIVES

As Radical Deepscale grows, the following principles will guide expansion decisions with respect to environmental responsibility:

  • Any new infrastructure will be evaluated against carbon-neutral or renewable-powered providers before provisioning.
  • AI inference partners will be assessed for published commitments to data center efficiency and renewable energy sourcing.
  • Hardware procurement will prioritize longevity, repairability, and energy efficiency ratings over specification maximalism.
  • Open-source contributions will be maintained and expanded, reducing duplication of research effort across the broader scientific community.
  • Augmented and virtual reality applications — including the planned Unreal Engine ArcLake AR platform — will be architected for offline-capable, low-bandwidth operation wherever feasible.

01 · OVERVIEW

ABOUT RADICAL DEEPSCALE

Radical Deepscale LLC is an independent science, engineering, and artificial intelligence research company operating at the intersection of aerospace systems, computational physics, and advanced AI architecture. The organization is built on a lean model — a single founder in active collaboration with freelancers and researchers who share a commitment to rigorous, long-horizon scientific work.

Our product ecosystem — the DART Meadow platform — spans interactive engineering visualization, AI-driven exploration, materials science, satellite tracking, physics simulation, and a suite of computational design tools. Every product is built to be open, accessible, and designed to endure rather than be replaced on short cycles.

This report documents our environmental commitments and establishes a baseline pledge that grows with the company. We believe that building systems that last is itself a form of sustainability.

Radical Deepscale's entire public platform is served as static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript via GitHub Pages — one of the lowest-energy web infrastructure models commercially available. There are no always-on application servers, no managed database instances, and no containerized service fleets running around the clock.

Content is distributed through GitHub's global CDN, serving assets from nodes geographically nearest to each visitor — minimizing transmission distance, latency, and the energy cost per request. The absence of server-side rendering eliminates the compute-per-request energy overhead entirely.

All AI inference is routed through third-party API providers (Anthropic, Pollinations) that operate their own infrastructure sustainability programs. Radical Deepscale itself generates no inference compute load independently.

03 · PHILOSOPHY

LONG-RUN DESIGN ETHIC

Planned obsolescence is one of the most significant contributors to technological waste. Radical Deepscale's engineering philosophy is structured in direct opposition to this: every tool is built with permanence as a design constraint. Single-file HTML applications are self-contained, dependency-light, and executable on any device with a browser — with no framework churn, no version rot, no external infrastructure to deprecate.

Our use of vanilla web technologies (HTML5 Canvas, WebGL via Three.js, the Web Audio API, WebXR) means applications age gracefully — browsers maintain backward compatibility with these standards for decades. A tool built today remains usable without re-deployment or maintenance.

This model reduces not only computational waste but also the human energy cost of perpetual re-engineering — freeing research bandwidth for meaningful scientific work rather than maintenance overhead.

04 · COMMITMENTS

SUSTAINABILITY PLEDGES

🌱ZERO SERVER FOOTPRINT

We pledge to maintain a serverless, static-first architecture as our deployment standard — keeping direct compute emissions at zero for all public-facing products.

♻️LONGEVITY OVER REPLACEMENT

Products are built to last. We commit to iterative improvement rather than full rewrites — preserving existing user value while minimizing the waste of throw-away development cycles.

🔓OPEN ACCESS

Tools are published openly on GitHub Pages with no paywalled infrastructure. Open access reduces duplication of effort across the research community.

🧠RESPONSIBLE AI USE

AI inference is used purposefully and routed through providers committed to responsible and efficient compute operations. No bulk generation or data harvesting.

📡LEAN DEPENDENCY STACK

We minimize third-party library dependencies to reduce supply-chain complexity, bandwidth consumption, and the energy cost of unnecessary asset delivery.

🔬SCIENCE FOR DURABILITY

Research into materials science, alloy engineering, and aerospace systems is oriented toward improving performance and lifespan — directly reducing replacement rates and material waste.

05 · PRINCIPLES

OPERATING PRINCIPLES

P.01

Optimization over consumption. Every system we build is analyzed for efficiency first. The LEATR framework's core mandate — research, reduce, and optimize — applies to our own operational footprint as much as to our engineering outputs.

P.02

Minimal viable infrastructure. We run the minimum infrastructure required to serve our mission. A single-person company does not need a data center. GitHub Pages, public CDNs, and community APIs are sufficient and proportionate.

P.03

Knowledge as a renewable resource. Documentation, open repositories, and published research compound in value over time. We invest in knowledge assets that remain useful indefinitely without additional energy expenditure.

P.04

Freelancer and community model. Radical Deepscale operates with freelancers and contributors of shared interest — a distributed, low-overhead model that eliminates the environmental cost of maintaining physical office infrastructure.

P.05

Materials research aligned with sustainability. Our alloy research programs (Hercules, T11-ZC, Hypersonic Edge) are oriented toward high-performance, long-lifespan materials — directly reducing replacement frequency and the energy cost of manufacturing cycles.

06 · FORWARD

LONG-TERM ENVIRONMENTAL ROADMAP

As Radical Deepscale grows, environmental accountability grows with it. Near-term commitments include tracking and publishing the carbon footprint of any infrastructure expanded beyond static hosting; evaluating AI inference providers on their published sustainability metrics; and contributing to open-source tooling that reduces energy overhead in scientific visualization and simulation.

In the medium term, we intend to formalize partnerships with research institutions and engineering communities that share a long-run optimization mandate — building a network of aligned contributors who prioritize durability, efficiency, and scientific rigor over rapid-cycle product turnover.

The DART Meadow platform itself serves as an ongoing proof of concept: that a single developer working responsibly with lean tools can produce a rich and functional engineering ecosystem — with a carbon footprint that scales far slower than its scientific output.

The USDZ format is a zip archive containing a primary .usda or .usdc file. Apple's AR Quick Look and most iOS apps expect the USDA text format with specific metadata (upAxis = "Y", AR plane anchoring, and a valid defaultPrim). Nomad Sculpt's "OpenUSD Apple" export preset produces a compatible file; the raw "OpenUSD" preset generates binary USDC which often fails in non-Nomad applications.
